Questions about this set

Is the Credential of Ministry legally valid?
Yes. It's the official certificate of your ordination with the Universal Life Church and is the document most clerks ask to see.
Do I need this to officiate?
Your ordination is valid without it. You need printed proof only if your county, venue, or couple asks for it — many do.
Can I order for someone else?
Credentials are issued only in the ordained minister's legal name. They can get ordained free in minutes.
