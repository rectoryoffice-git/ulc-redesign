How to officiate a wedding in Texas

Officiant requirements, ceremony rules, and marriage license deadlines for the Lone Star State — in one place.

By the ULC Marriage Law TeamLast reviewed Oct 12, 2026Sources: Tex. Fam. Code §§ 2.003–2.203
Quick answer

Yes — ULC ministers can legally officiate weddings in Texas. Online ordination is recognized, no state registration is required, and you don't need to live in Texas. Some county clerks may ask to see proof of ordination, so bring your credentials.

Texas FAQ

Can I officiate in Texas if I live in another state?
Yes. Texas has no residency requirement for officiants.
Do the couple need to be Texas residents?
No. Couples from any state or country can marry in Texas. Both must generally be 18 or older.
Can the 72-hour waiting period be waived?
Yes, in some cases — for example, active-duty military, or couples who completed an approved premarital education course. A judge can also grant a waiver.
