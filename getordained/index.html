Questions, answered

Getting ordained online: the short answers

How do I get ordained online?
Fill out the free application: your name and email, then your state and a password. You're ordained as a Universal Life Church minister the moment you finish, usually in about five minutes.
Is getting ordained online legal?
Yes. ULC ordinations are recognized in nearly every U.S. state, and the ULC has defended its ministers in federal court. A few states and counties have extra rules, which our state marriage law guides explain.
How much does it cost to get ordained?
Nothing. Ordination is free and never expires. Printed credentials are optional and start at $11.99.
How long does it take to get ordained?
About five minutes. Your ordination takes effect immediately; printed documents ship in a few business days, or overnight with the Emergency Wedding Kit.
How do I get ordained to marry someone?
Here's how to get ordained to marry someone you love: apply free online, then check the marriage laws for the state where the wedding takes place. That's it.
Can I officiate a friend's wedding in any state?
In nearly every state, yes. Check the marriage laws for the state where the wedding takes place, since some counties ask to see proof of ordination.
