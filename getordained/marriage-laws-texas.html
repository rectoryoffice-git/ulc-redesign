Can I officiate a wedding in Texas?

Texas marriage laws for wedding officiants: who can officiate, ceremony rules, and the license deadlines, in one place.

By the Get Ordained Marriage Law TeamReviewed Oct 28, 2026Source: Tex. Fam. Code §§ 2.003–2.203
Quick answerYes, you can officiate a wedding in Texas with online ordination. No state registration, no witnesses, and you don't need to live in Texas. Some county clerks may ask to see proof of ordination.

Texas FAQ

Can I officiate in Texas if I live in another state?
Yes. Texas has no residency requirement for officiants.
Can the 72-hour waiting period be waived?
In some cases, for example for active-duty military, or couples who completed an approved premarital course.

Laws change. Confirm with the county clerk before the ceremony. This is general information, not legal advice.
