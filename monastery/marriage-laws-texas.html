Marriage laws by state · Texas

How to perform a wedding in Texas

What Texas requires of ministers, the ceremony and the marriage license.

By the ULC Monastery Marriage Law TeamReviewed Oct 28, 2026Source: Tex. Fam. Code §§ 2.003–2.203
Quick answerYes, ULC ministers can perform weddings in Texas. Online ordination is recognized, no state registration is needed, and no witnesses are required. Some county clerks may ask to see proof of ordination.

Requirements for ministers

Online ordination recognizedYes
Minimum age18
Texas residency requiredNo
State registrationNot required

The ceremony

Minister must be presentYes
WitnessesNone required

The marriage license
