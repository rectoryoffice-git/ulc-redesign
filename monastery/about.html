Is the Universal Life Church legit?

Yes, and legally backed

Nationwide

Recognized in nearly every state

ULC ministers can legally officiate weddings in nearly every U.S. state. A few, such as Virginia, have extra rules, and our guides spell them out.

Federal court · 2023

Defended in Tennessee

After the ULC sued over a 2019 Tennessee law, the district attorneys it sued agreed in federal court not to challenge marriages performed by ULC ministers.

ULC Monastery Storehouse v. Nabors, M.D. Tenn.
Federal court · 2002

Online ordination upheld

A federal court struck down Utah's ban on officiants ordained online, and permanently barred the state from enforcing it.

Universal Life Church v. Utah, D. Utah
