Questions, answered

Becoming a minister: the short answers

How do I become a minister?
Fill out the free ordination form: your name and email, then your state and a password. You become an ordained minister of the Universal Life Church Monastery as soon as you finish.
What can an ordained minister do?
Officiate weddings, lead funerals and memorial services, perform baptisms and blessings, and start your own ministry.
Is ULC ordination legal?
Yes. ULC ordinations are recognized in nearly every U.S. state, and the ULC has defended its ministers in federal court. See the legal record.
Does it cost anything?
No. Ordination is free and never expires. Credentials and church supplies are optional.
